Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

Who is responsible for your data?
Keyur Gohil, acting as an individual, is the data controller for this private application.

1. How to contact me

Email spvcalculator.support@oopahone.com for privacy questions or requests. This mailbox is not monitored daily, so a response may be delayed. Please do not include passwords, authentication tokens, or unnecessary sensitive information.

2. Information the app may process

  • Account information, including email address, authentication identifier, display name, access status and role.
  • Property-planning information, including titles, property details, listing links, viewing dates, purchase figures, calculations and shared notes.
  • Expense information, including amounts, dates, categories, descriptions, notes and receipt files you choose to upload.
  • Forecast inputs and other app preferences stored on your device.
  • Push-notification subscription information, such as a device endpoint and encryption keys, when you enable notifications.
  • Technical information needed to provide and protect the service, such as session information, request logs, error information, browser storage and cache data.

3. Why information is used

Information is used to authenticate approved users, operate the shared property workspace, synchronise records between authorised users, store private receipts, deliver notifications you request, maintain security, investigate faults and administer access.

4. Lawful bases

The main lawful basis is legitimate interests: operating and securing a limited-access tool for approved collaborators. Where processing is necessary to provide access requested by a user, it may also be necessary for performance of an agreement. Device notification permission is requested before push notifications are enabled. Information may also be processed where necessary to comply with a legal obligation.

5. Local device storage and cookies

The app uses browser storage, IndexedDB, a service worker and cached files so it can retain calculations, preferences and selected receipts and work offline. Supabase may store authentication-session information in the browser. The app does not currently use advertising or analytics cookies.

Removing browser data or uninstalling the app may erase the local copy, but it does not necessarily delete information already synchronised to cloud services.

6. Service providers and recipients

  • Supabase provides authentication, database storage and access control.
  • Cloudflare Workers and R2 provide protected receipt-file processing and storage.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process ordinary web-server request information.
  • jsDelivr delivers the Supabase browser library.
  • Your browser or operating-system push service processes notification subscription and delivery information if you enable notifications.

Information is shared with other approved workspace users where that is part of the feature—for example shared properties, expenses and notes. It is not sold and is not used for behavioural advertising.

7. International transfers

Some providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where UK data-protection law requires safeguards, reliance is placed on the provider's applicable transfer mechanism and contractual protections.

8. Retention and deletion

Cloud information is generally retained while the workspace or account remains in use and until it is deleted, no longer needed, or removal is requested and can lawfully be completed. Provider backups and security logs may remain for a limited period under provider retention schedules. Local information remains on a device until it is deleted by the user, cleared by the browser, or removed when the app is uninstalled.

9. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you rights to access, correct, erase or restrict your personal data, object to processing, receive portable data and complain about how information is used. Identity may need to be verified before a request is completed.

You may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. Please contact me first if you are comfortable doing so, as this may allow the issue to be resolved.

10. Security and your responsibilities

Reasonable technical controls are used, including authentication, workspace roles, database row-level security and private receipt storage. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your credentials private, use a secure device and report suspected unauthorised access promptly.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app, providers or legal requirements change. The updated date above will be changed, and material new uses of personal data will be brought to affected users' attention where required.
